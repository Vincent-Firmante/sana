The Resort

Welcome to Canyon Cove

Canyon Cove Hotel & Spa is a seaside resort in Nasugbu, Batangas, set along a sheltered cove with views of the bay and the surrounding hills. Red-roofed buildings, palm-lined walkways and a sweeping lagoon-style pool make it a relaxing getaway for families, couples, barkadas and corporate groups.

Whether you want to swim, try a water sport, unwind at the spa or simply watch the sunset by the beach, everything is within a short walk of your room.
